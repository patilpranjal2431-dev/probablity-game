Why Edge Lab Separates Skill (EV) from Coins (Luck + Skill)

In quantitative trading, poker, and risk management, judging a strategy solely by short-term P&L (coins won) is misleading because realized outcomes combine decision quality with random variance:

Realized Coin Profit = Decision Expected Value (Skill) + Outcome Variance (Luck)

Over dozens of hands, variance dominates coin balances. A player who makes mathematically sound calls can lose coins on bad showdowns, while a reckless player can get lucky and briefly top the coin leaderboard. Edge Lab tracks both leaderboards side by side so you can separate process from outcome.

Metric 1 — Coins Leaderboard
Luck + Skill

Ranked by your audited wallet balance. Reflects both your betting decisions and random card/die showdowns. High variance over small sample sizes.

Metric 2 — Cumulative EV Leaderboard
Decision Skill

Sum of the exact Expected Value of every action you took, computed at the moment you acted before the opponent's hidden hand is revealed. Eliminates showdown/runout luck.

Metric 3 — Decision Regret
Best EV − Taken EV

Always ≥ 0. Measures how many expected coins you gave up compared to the mathematically optimal action at that exact spot. Zero regret means optimal play even with bad cards.

How Edge Lab Computes Decision EV & Regret

After every hand finishes in the Live Lobby, the server evaluates each legal choice you had from your observation point (pot, to_call, and exact showdown equity):

Action Formula (Incremental EV from Decision Point)
FOLD EV = 0.00 (no extra coins risked; prior pot chips are sunk)
CALL EV = equity × (pot + to_call) − to_call
CHECK EV = equity × pot
BET b Exact expectation over opponent's remaining hands: fold (+pot) when opp_equity < b / (pot + 2b), else showdown (+(pot + b) win, +pot/2 tie, −b loss)

Concrete Example: Winning EV vs. Winning Coins

Suppose in 1-Card Poker the pot is 40 coins and your opponent bets 20 coins (so pot = 40, to_call = 20). You hold a King (equity ≈ 89.2%):

  • EV(CALL) = 0.892 × (40 + 20) − 20 = +33.53 coins.
  • EV(FOLD) = 0.00 coins.
  • If you CALL, your Regret = 0.00. Even if your opponent happens to turn over an Ace and you lose 20 more coins on the ledger, the Skill Leaderboard still credits you with +33.53 EV!
  • Conversely, if you FOLD because you were afraid of losing, you lock in 0.00 EV and incur 33.53 coins of regret.
